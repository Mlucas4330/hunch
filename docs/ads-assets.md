# Ad assets

Every string that is live in the `Hunch BR Search` campaign, in the language it runs in.
**The reasoning is not here.** What an ad may say, why these four themes and why the fifth is
paused all live in [ads.md](ads.md); this file is the record of what was typed in, so that a line
in the account can be traced to a decision without opening the UI.

Four limits bind everything below and they are Google's: **30 characters a headline, 90 a
description, 25 a sitelink text, 35 a sitelink description, 25 a callout, 15 a path.** Portuguese
spends characters faster than English does, so a line rewritten in a hurry is the one that gets
truncated in the auction.

**Accents are required and dashes are not allowed.** That is the same rule the product holds its
own pt-BR output to: a technical term the market uses in English stays in English, the accented
characters stay accented, and no line uses a dash, an ellipsis character or a curly quote. See
[i18n.md](i18n.md).

## Responsive search ads

One ad per ad group, fifteen headlines and four descriptions each, **nothing pinned**. Pinning a
headline to position one buys consistency and costs the auction most of what it had to work with,
and at this volume there is nothing to consistently protect yet.

**The price sitelink is the one to check after any repricing.** It carried R$147 for months after
the packs moved to R$97 and R$247, which made it the only false claim the product had in public.
A sitelink is the easiest asset to forget because it is written once at campaign level and never
appears in the ad editor afterwards.

- Créditos não expiram
- Pix, cartão ou boleto

**No structured snippets.** The header list is a fixed enum and none of its entries describes a
measurement, so the honest ones would all read as feature lists of something this product is not.

## What is deliberately not written here

- **No price in a headline or description.** R$97 belongs on the page where the pack card explains
  what it buys, and a price in a headline invites the click that came to compare rather than to
  measure. It appears in one sitelink and nowhere else.
- **No countdown, no scarcity, no "ultimas vagas".** Nothing here is scarce: an analysis costs a
  couple of reais to produce and the credits do not expire.
- **No number about what a fix produces**, in any asset, ever. That is the invariant every surface
  of this product is held to, and the ad is the surface where breaking it is most tempting. See
  [invariants.md](invariants.md#a-delta-is-arithmetic-between-two-measurements-never-a-result-attributed-to-a-change).
- **No builder named in a claim.** A tool name may address the reader, as in "Fez o site com IA?",
  and may never carry an assertion about what that tool produces.
